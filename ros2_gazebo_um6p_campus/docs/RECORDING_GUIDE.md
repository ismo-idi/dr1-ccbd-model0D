# Recording and presentation media

Use the manual two-terminal launch instructions in the root README. The automated verifier intentionally pauses and closes its scene and is unsuitable for uninterrupted recording. Wait for the paused campus to load, frame the view with the mouse, start your desktop recorder, then start the demo in Terminal B. Keep the large top-right clearance panel visible. Stop recording after both land; stop Gazebo in Terminal A. Relaunch to repeat deterministically.

Demo 1 recording is in `demo/demo_1_no_obstacle/media/videos/`. Demo 2 recording is in `demo/demo_2_static_sphere/media/videos/`. These are the user's supplied originals, copied without transcoding. Their recorded date, exact Gazebo revision and complete test conditions are not inferred from the files. They illustrate the scene; automated claims refer to separate result/trace records.

Each scenario's `media/screenshots/` contains actual Gazebo captures selected for reports. Captions should say "deterministic kinematic demonstration" and state that clearance uses conservative geometric bounds. Do not describe these pictures as validation of aerodynamics, a flight controller, TCC or the numerical campaign. The scene reconstruction is inspired by the campus photographs.
