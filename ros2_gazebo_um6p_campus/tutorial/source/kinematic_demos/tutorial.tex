\textbf{Purpose.} Learn to implement deterministic ascent, travel, yielding, spherical-obstacle bypass and landing. These are kinematic demonstrations with explicit geometric clearance checks. They do not model aerodynamic forces, rotor thrust, tracking error or physical flight control.

Research context: \emph{Constraint Consensus-Based Dynamics for Decentralized Path Planning in UAV Swarms}. This showcase does not implement TCC, Sun--Sun, consensus or an optimization solver. Its geometry and paths are separate from the numerical campaign.
